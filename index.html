You reject thousands of people who wanted to work for you.

Then you send a template that makes them like you less. Cushion plugs into your existing applicant tracking system and turns each rejection into a warm, on-brand note with a real reward, so the people who chose your brand stay customers instead of walking away.

The questions your legal and finance teams will ask

Is putting an offer inside a rejection email legal?
A rejection is a service message; a promotional reward is marketing, which under UK GDPR and PECR needs a lawful basis and a clear opt-out. Cushion is designed to support that, with consent captured at the point of application, a visible opt-out on every message, and a full audit trail. Confirm the approach with your own counsel; we provide a DPA and a template DPIA. This is not legal advice.
What happens to our margin if lots of people redeem?
You set the reward and its terms. Your worst case is redemptions multiplied by your reward cost, and it only fires on people you would otherwise have lost.
Doesn't a discount read as insulting after a rejection?
The message leads with a genuine thank-you and the reward sits underneath it, never as the headline. You set the tone per stage.
What stops the codes ending up on a voucher site?
Every code is unique to the applicant and single-use, tied to the email we sent it to, with an expiry and an optional cap.
